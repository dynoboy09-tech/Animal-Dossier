Colobus Monkey

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Habitats: Forest and Savanna lands

Common name: Colobus Monkey

This is a species of Colobus Monkey called the Colobus Guereza Monkey. They have a mix of white and black fur in adult hood but when first born they have white fur and due to there fur they have been sought for there fur pelts. Zoo's have now been keeping these monkeys but before wouldn't due to there special diet. They are only leaf eaters and will only eat exclusively a type of gastric leaf fermentation.
