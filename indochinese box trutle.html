Indochinese Box turtle

  • Conservation Status: Critcally Endangered
  • Habitat: Tropical and Subtropical forests of Vietnam, Lao, and Southern China.
  • Common name: Indochinese Box turtle
  • Kingdom: Animalia
  • Phylum: Chordata
  • Subphylum: Vertebrata
  • Class: Reptilia
  • Subclass: Diapsida
  • Order: testudines
  • Suborder: Cryptodira
  • Family: Ceomydidae
  • Genus: Cuora
  • Species: Cuora Galbinifrons
  • Physical Profile:

This turtle is names the Indochinese Box Turtle or Flowerback Boc Turtle because of its pattern on its back looks like a flower with its bright orange colors all over its body.

They are omnivores they eat mostly insects, vegetables, and some fruits.

Like most turtles are shy and reclusive forest-dwellers they like to hide under any foilage they find rather then being in the open.

These small turtles communication to others by doing a non-visual physical cue and body movements.

The Indochinese Box Turtle will dig in a small whole in soft soil and lay up to 1-3 eggs in the hole then covers them up. The parents provide no care to the eggs and let them incubate in the ground.

After they hatch they hide from every things and will only eat small insects. After in juvenile stage they start to semi explore and their diet shifts to be more omnivorous.

Once they become a adult they are 15-20 cm or 6-8 in length they live for a long time and love their cool high-elevation but due to their slow reporduction they aren critcally endangered.

Their preadators are large forest carnivores, predatory birds and snakes.
